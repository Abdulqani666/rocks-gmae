Rules

  1. Each player has 12 rocks on intersections.
  2. First move: White must move a rock downward only.
  3. Each rock moves 1 step in 4 directions to an empty intersection.
  4. To eat: jump over an enemy — land beyond it. Enemy removed.
  5. After eating you can chain eat or stop.
  6. After White's first move, if Black eats, White must eat back (11 vs 11).
  7. A rock can jump over a friendly if space beyond is empty.
  8. Single rocks cannot jump over double rocks.
  9. Reach opponent's back row → Double Rock (gold ring).
  10. Double rock eaten only by another double rock.
  11. Neither player may make a move that leaves opponent with zero moves.
  12. Opponent left with 1 rock → you win!
